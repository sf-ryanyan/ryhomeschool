## The Service · 业务介绍

**RY Homes** is a bilingual real estate practice in the San Francisco Bay Area,
focused on one thing most agents treat as an afterthought: **getting a family
into the right school district, on the housing side.**

Every year families move to the Bay Area for its public schools — from other
US states, from overseas, or from a weaker district nearby. They meet the same
wall: the schools are excellent, but the information is scattered, attendance
boundaries are counter-intuitive, and the strong rentals are gone before they
are ever listed.

The schools are not the hard part. **The information is.** That gap is the
whole practice.

> **RY Homes 是旧金山湾区的中英双语地产服务,专注一件大多数经纪人顺带处理的事:
> 把家庭送进对的学区 —— 房子这一段。**
>
> 每年都有大量家庭为了公立教育搬来湾区 —— 从美国外州、从海外、或者从本地较弱的
> 学区搬过来。他们撞上的是同一堵墙:学校很好,但信息零散、就读边界反直觉、好房源
> 往往还没公开挂牌就租掉了。
>
> 难的从来不是学校,**是信息。** 这个信息差,就是这项业务存在的理由。

### Scope boundary · 服务边界

This is a **real estate practice**, not an education consultancy. School
enrollment is filed by parents through each district's own official process and
decided by the district under its own rules. Nothing here promises an admission
outcome, and immigration, legal and tax questions go to the appropriate
licensed professionals.

> 这是一项**地产经纪服务**,不是教育咨询。入学注册由家长依据各学区官方流程自行
> 办理,结果由学区依其规定审核决定。本业务**不代办入学申请,不承诺任何入学结果**;
> 涉及移民、法律与税务的问题,请咨询相应的持牌专业人士。

## Data sources · 数据来源

School ratings come from **GreatSchools**, rankings from the **US News 2026-27
Best High Schools** list, district and attendance-boundary facts from each
district's own published materials, and rent ranges from public market data.
Every figure on the page was verified against its source before publication.

Data compiled **October 2026**. Ratings, rankings and rents change over time —
always confirm with the district and the original source.

> 学校评分引自 **GreatSchools**,排名引自 **US News 2026-27 全美最佳高中榜**,
> 学区与就读边界信息引自各学区官方发布材料,租金区间为公开市场行情整理。
> 页面上的每一个数字在发布前都已逐项核对来源。
>
> 数据整理截至 **2026 年 10 月**。评分、排名与租金随时间变化,请以各学区与原始
> 来源的最新发布为准。
